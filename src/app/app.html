Research Interests

Software Engineering for AI (SE4AI) / ML Systems

My primary direction is the reliability, testing, deployment, and monitoring of ML-enabled software. I want to study how software architecture, MLOps, and model and version management can support maintainable AI applications in real operating environments.

Computer Vision / Robust Visual Systems

My existing research focuses on iris recognition under difficult imaging conditions. I remain interested in image segmentation, biometric recognition, and deep learning, including how to evaluate robust visual models and integrate them into reliable applications.

AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE)

I am also interested in using AI, machine learning, and large language models to support software testing, bug detection, fault localization, and program repair.
