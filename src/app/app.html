Research Interests

Software Engineering for AI (SE4AI) / ML Systems

I want to study what it takes to keep ML-based applications working after they leave the lab: testing them, deploying them, monitoring changes, and managing model versions.

Computer Vision / Robust Visual Systems

My undergraduate work examined iris recognition when images are blurred, occluded, or unevenly lit. I am interested in image segmentation and biometric recognition, including how to evaluate these models when image quality varies.

AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE)

I would like to explore how AI tools can help with software testing, bug detection, fault localization, and program repair.
